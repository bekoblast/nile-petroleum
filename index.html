Welcome to Nilepet

Sudan's Trusted Partner in Energy & Petroleum

Established by the Government of the Republic of Sudan, Nile Petroleum Company Limited (Nilepet) is mandated to develop and manage the country's oil and gas resources for sustainable national growth. We operate across the entire value chain — from exploration and production to refining, distribution, and retail.

What We Do

Comprehensive Petroleum Services

We deliver an end-to-end energy solution for Sudan — covering exploration, production, refining, logistics, retail and value-added services for industrial and consumer markets.

Exploration & Production

Upstream operations across multiple blocks in Sudan — geological surveys, seismic studies, drilling, and reservoir management.

Refining & Processing

Conversion of crude oil into refined products — gasoline, diesel, kerosene, jet fuel, LPG, and lubricant base oils.

Distribution & Logistics

Nationwide pipeline, marine, rail and road transport, supported by strategic depots and storage terminals.

Retail Fuel Network

Aviation & Marine Fuels

Our Commitment

Health, Safety, Environment & Sustainability

Energy responsibility is at the core of how we operate. Our HSE & ESG framework drives lower emissions, safer workplaces, and meaningful contribution to the communities we serve.

Environmental Stewardship

We invest in cleaner-fuel production, methane-leak detection, biodiversity studies and rehabilitation programs across all our operating sites.

Occupational Safety

Zero-harm culture backed by ISO 45001 systems, regular drills, contractor management, and a transparent incident-reporting platform.

Community Impact

Education programs, primary healthcare clinics, vocational training and clean-water projects in oil-producing communities across Sudan.
