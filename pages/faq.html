Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about our products, services, careers, and corporate operations.

What is Nile Petroleum Company Limited (Nilepet)?

Nilepet is the national oil company of the Republic of Sudan, established to develop and manage the country's oil & gas resources. We operate across the entire petroleum value chain — exploration, production, refining, distribution and retail.

How can I locate the nearest Nilepet fuel station?

Visit our Fuel Stations page and use the search filter by state, service type, or station name. The interactive map shows real-time station locations with services offered.

Do you offer bulk fuel supply for businesses?

Yes. We provide bulk supply agreements for industrial, transport, aviation, marine, and government customers. Please contact our commercial team via the Contact page with your requirements (volume, product, location, frequency).

How do I file a complaint or report an issue?

Use our bilingual complaint management system available on the Contact page. Select "Complaint / Feedback" as the topic. Every complaint is assigned a tracking number and acknowledged within 24 hours. We are committed to resolving all valid complaints within 7 working days.

Where can I find financial reports and investor materials?

Our full library of annual reports, quarterly earnings, governance documents, and ESG disclosures is available in the Investor Relations section.

How can I apply for a job at Nilepet?

All open positions are listed on our Careers page. You can apply directly online by uploading your CV and supporting documents. Our talent team reviews every application within 7 working days.

What HSE certifications does Nilepet hold?

Nilepet is certified under ISO 9001 (Quality Management), ISO 14001 (Environmental Management) and ISO 45001 (Occupational Health & Safety). Our HSE policies and performance data are published in our annual sustainability report.

Does Nilepet offer scholarships or community programs?

Yes — we run scholarship programs for engineering and STEM students, vocational training partnerships, primary healthcare clinics, and clean-water initiatives in oil-producing communities. Visit the HSE & ESG page for the full list of community programs.

How do I become a Nilepet supplier or vendor?

We welcome qualified suppliers across services, equipment, and consumables. Please email procurement@nilepet.sd with your company profile, certifications, and product/service catalog. Our procurement team will review and respond within 10 working days.

How can I subscribe to news and announcements?

Subscribe to our newsletter at the bottom of any page on the website. We publish operational updates, sustainability news, financial highlights, and corporate announcements monthly.
