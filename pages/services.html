Our Services

An integrated portfolio of services across the petroleum value chain — engineered to deliver reliability, efficiency, and value for every customer we serve.

Upstream

Exploration & Production

From early-stage geological surveys to full-field development, our upstream teams deploy modern seismic, drilling, and reservoir technology to unlock Sudan's hydrocarbon potential responsibly.

We currently operate 12 concession blocks in partnership with leading regional and international operators.

3D & 4D Seismic acquisition Reservoir simulation & modeling Directional & offshore drilling Production optimization
Midstream

Refining & Processing

Our refining facilities convert crude oil into a diverse product slate — gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, kerosene, LPG, fuel oil and lubricant base oils — meeting national demand and supporting export trade.

The Khartoum Refinery operates with a capacity of 130,000 bbl/day, with Phase-II expansion underway.

Atmospheric & vacuum distillation Catalytic reforming & FCC units Hydrotreating to Euro V Lubricant base-oil production
Logistics

Distribution & Logistics

Sudan's largest fuel-distribution network connects our refineries to customers via 1,400+ km of pipeline, 15 strategic depots, and a managed fleet of road and rail tankers.

Real-time SCADA and IoT monitoring ensure asset integrity, supply continuity, and safety across the network.

Cross-country pipeline ops Storage at strategic depots Road tanker GPS tracking Marine bunkering at Port Sudan
Specialty

Aviation & Marine Fuels

Specialised fuels and lubricants for aviation, marine, and industrial customers — backed by international quality standards (JIG-1530, ISO 8217) and dedicated handling infrastructure at major airports and ports.

Jet A-1 supply & into-plane Avgas 100LL distribution IFO & MGO bunkers Industrial fuel oil
