About Nile Petroleum

Sudan's national oil company — built to develop, manage, and protect the country's hydrocarbon resources for the prosperity of all Sudanese.

Company Profile

Two Decades of Energy Leadership

Nile Petroleum Company Limited (Nilepet) was incorporated by the Government of Sudan to oversee the country's hydrocarbon value chain — from upstream exploration and production to midstream pipelines, refining, and downstream retail distribution.

Today, Nilepet is the largest fully-integrated oil & gas company in Sudan, operating across 18 states, employing over 3,800 professionals, and partnering with leading regional and international energy firms.

Established 2004
12 active concessions
450+ retail stations
ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001

Our Story

Journey of Milestones

2004

Establishment

Nilepet incorporated as Sudan's national oil company by Presidential decree to manage the country's petroleum sector.

2008

First Concession Operations

Launched upstream operations in Block 6, marking the company's transition into active exploration and production.

2012

Refining Capacity Expansion

Khartoum Refinery Phase-I expansion completed, raising daily processing capacity to 100,000 bbl/day.

2018

Retail Network Launch

The Nilepet branded service-station network goes live with 150 stations nationwide.

2023

Sustainability Strategy 2030

Published flagship ESG roadmap with targets across emissions, biodiversity, and community investment.

2026

Digital Transformation

Launched enterprise digital platform connecting upstream sensors, ERP, retail and customer experience.
