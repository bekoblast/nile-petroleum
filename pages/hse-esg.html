Our Framework

Three Pillars of Action

Environment · Social · Governance — each anchored in measurable targets and external assurance.

Environment

Methane-leak detection, biodiversity studies, cleaner-fuel production, climate-risk modeling and rehabilitation programs.

Social

Community education, primary healthcare, vocational training, women-in-energy initiatives and local-content procurement.

Governance

Independent board oversight, anti-corruption policies, whistle-blower protections, transparent reporting and audits.

Safety Culture

Zero-Harm Operations

Our safety management system is certified to ISO 45001 and integrates contractor management, root-cause analysis, behaviour-based safety, and continuous improvement.

Every employee — from the boardroom to the wellsite — has the authority and obligation to stop unsafe work.
